How to play

  • Click / tap the floor to walk there.
  • Click the chair (work), the armchair (read), the bed (sleep) or the red book (portfolio).
  • Drag to look around, scroll / pinch to zoom.
  • Slider = time of day; ▶ auto lets time run (1 hour ≈ 10 s).
  • 🪟 opens the window, 🗓 makes the character follow the clock, 🎥 makes the camera follow the character.
  • Press H or ? to show or hide this help, Esc to close the book.
